Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026 Developer: Dhruv Dhameliya Contact: dhruvdhameliya.dev@gmail.com

Overview

Attendance Analyzer ("the Extension") is a client-side Chrome extension designed to assist students with real-time attendance calculations, safe leave forecasts, and simulation of co-curricular (Yellow) and medical (Blue) concession claims.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the data handling practices of the extension.

1. Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Data Collection: Attendance Analyzer does not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Local Processing Only: All attendance parsing, percentage evaluations, leave threshold calculations, and simulator projections are performed entirely locally within your browser sandbox.
  • No External Servers: The extension communicates with zero external telemetry, tracking, or remote backend servers.
  • Local Storage: Preferences (such as chosen goal thresholds, theme preferences, and pending form draft values) are stored strictly within Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and never leave your device.

2. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Used to persist user configuration locally (e.g. target percentage, compact layout mode, dark/light theme).
  • notifications: Used solely for optional local browser notifications (e.g. attendance drop alerts) if enabled by the user.
  • host_permissions (https://cue.christuniversity.in/*, https://espro.christuniversity.in:444/*): Restricted exclusively to the designated portal domains to inject the analyzer dashboard.

3. Third-Party Services

The settings and landing pages provide optional user-initiated support links (e.g., Razorpay) and contact email links. No personal browsing or attendance data is ever shared with or transmitted to these external providers.

4. User Control & Data Deletion

Users can clear all stored extension data at any time by clicking "Reset to Default Settings" in the extension options page or by uninstalling the extension from Chrome.
