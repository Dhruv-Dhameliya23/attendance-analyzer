Attendance Analyzer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Attendance Analyzer is an independent, unofficial browser extension that helps students understand attendance information, calculate attendance, simulate supported claim adjustments, and predict possible future attendance outcomes.

Privacy at a glance: Attendance information read by the extension is processed locally in the browser for the extension's attendance features. Attendance data is not sold or transferred to the developer's servers for advertising or unrelated purposes.

1. Information the Extension Accesses

To provide its core functionality, Attendance Analyzer may read attendance-related information displayed on supported student portal pages. This can include attendance percentages, conducted classes or hours, attended hours, missed hours, subject names, and other attendance-related values needed for calculations.

The extension may also access information needed to calculate or simulate supported attendance adjustments, such as Yellow Form and Blue Form scenarios, where that information is available on the supported page.

2. Information Stored Locally

Attendance Analyzer may use Chrome's local extension storage to save user preferences and settings, such as predictor values, notification preferences, display preferences, and other configuration needed for the extension to work consistently across sessions.

Local storage is used to provide the extension's attendance tracking, calculation, prediction, and customization features. Stored settings remain on the user's browser unless the user removes the extension data or Chrome otherwise manages that storage.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by Attendance Analyzer is used only to:

  • Display attendance information in the extension.
  • Calculate attendance percentages and related values.
  • Provide subject-wise attendance analysis.
  • Simulate supported Yellow Form and Blue Form adjustments.
  • Predict possible attendance outcomes from user-selected scenarios.
  • Save extension preferences and provide optional attendance notifications.

4. Data Sharing and Sale

Attendance Analyzer does not sell, rent, or transfer attendance information, personal information, or locally stored extension data to third parties for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

The extension does not intentionally transmit attendance information to the developer's own servers. The extension's core attendance calculations are designed to be performed locally in the user's browser.

5. Personal, Health, Financial, and Authentication Information

Attendance Analyzer does not intentionally collect passwords, PINs, authentication credentials, payment card information, financial statements, or personal communications.

The extension does not require medical diagnoses or medical histories for its core functionality. A Blue Form simulation does not by itself mean that the extension collects health information; the extension only processes the attendance-related information required for its calculation features.

6. Web Pages and Browsing Activity

The extension accesses supported attendance pages only to provide its disclosed attendance analysis and prediction functionality. It does not intentionally build a history of the user's browsing activity, track unrelated websites, record keystrokes, record mouse movements, or monitor unrelated browsing behavior.

7. Notifications

If enabled by the user, notifications may be used to provide attendance-related alerts based on the extension's configured attendance settings. Notifications are part of the attendance management functionality and can be disabled through the extension settings.

8. Third-Party Services

Attendance Analyzer does not use attendance data for third-party advertising or data brokerage. The browser, operating system, Chrome Web Store, supported student portal, and any third-party resources used by a browser may have their own privacy practices and policies. Users should review those policies where applicable.

9. Security

The extension is designed to minimize data handling and process attendance calculations locally where possible. Users should keep Chrome and their operating system updated and use their normal account and device security practices.

10. Children's Privacy

Attendance Analyzer is intended as a student productivity tool and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for unrelated purposes.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's data practices, features, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the updated date shown at the top.

12. Independent and Unofficial Tool

Attendance Analyzer is an independent, unofficial tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to any university, college, educational institution, or student portal provider unless explicitly stated.

Attendance calculations and predictions are provided for informational and planning purposes only. Official attendance records and institutional decisions remain authoritative. The extension does not guarantee eligibility, claim approval, examination eligibility, or any particular final attendance result.

13. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Attendance Analyzer limits its handling of user data to the extension's disclosed single purpose: attendance tracking, calculation, simulation, and prediction. User data is not sold or transferred for personalized advertising, data brokerage, creditworthiness decisions, or other unrelated purposes.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please use the support contact provided on the Attendance Analyzer Chrome Web Store listing.
