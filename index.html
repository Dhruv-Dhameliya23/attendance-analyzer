Yellow Form Simulation

Simulate co-curricular concession claims (events, fests, sports, club activities) on your actual hours to see projected percentage gains before submitting official claims.

Blue Form (Medical) Adjustments

Forecast medical leave adjustments with university constraint verification. Check if your attendance meets the 75% Yellow threshold rule before applying for medical concessions.

Attendance Predictor & What-If Scenarios

Test future scenarios before you miss a lecture. Enter upcoming absences or planned attendance to calculate exact final percentages and safe margin buffers in real time.

Subject Deficit & Recovery Calculator

Categorizes each course by risk level (Critical, Warning, Safe). Calculates the exact number of consecutive classes you must attend to recover any low-attendance subject.
