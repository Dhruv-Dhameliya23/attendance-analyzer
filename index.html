Simulate additional missed or conducted classes using interactive sliders to instantly see the impact on overall and subject-wise percentages.

Choose your target goal (75%, 80%, 85%, or 90%) to see exactly how many upcoming classes you can safely miss without falling below your threshold.

Planning upcoming leaves? Enter your expected absences and total planned classes to project your end-of-semester attendance accurately.

Class Recovery Target

Need to get back on track? See the exact number of continuous classes you must attend in a row to safely restore your required attendance goal.
