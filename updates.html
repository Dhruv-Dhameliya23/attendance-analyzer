Version 4.0.0 Jalebi
Current Public Live Release
Major Release: Packed with individual subject calculations, Yellow & Blue claim form simulators, interactive guided tour, 100% offline privacy, multi-student profiles, and fluid page animations.
What's New in Version 4.0.0
  • Subject-Wise Calculation Engine: Real-time calculations for every single subject, showing safe leaves remaining and the exact number of classes needed to reach your target. New
  • Yellow & Blue Claim Simulators: Test how Medical/Co-curricular (Yellow) and Official Duty (Blue) attendance claims will boost your percentages before submitting forms. New
  • Subject Detailed View Breakdown: Clickable window displaying a complete summary of conducted, attended, and missed classes for each course. New
  • Interactive Welcome Tour: A helpful visual walkthrough on first install that guides you through all features step-by-step. New
  • 100% Offline & Private: Works entirely inside your browser on your device. No data is stored externally, ensuring complete personal privacy. 100% Offline
  • Leave & Claim Course Shortcuts: Links pending leave applications directly with your timetable for quick 1-click subject status checks. New
  • 900 Target Mode (90% Academic Excellence): High-achiever 90% attendance preset alongside 75%, 80%, and 85% goals for scholarship students. New Preset
  • Redesigned Settings Center: Modernized options with theme selection (Light, Dark, Portal-Match), notification preferences, and data backup. Upgraded
  • 5-Slot Multi-Student Profiles: Allows up to 5 students to use the extension on shared computers with separate profiles and automatic recognition. New
  • Protected 5-Second Hold-to-Reset: Safety button with an animated progress sweep preventing accidental resets. Safe Reset
  • Instant Portal Compatibility: Smooth automatic updates across both CUE and ESPRO portal pages whenever you switch tabs. Instant Sync
  • Sleek Design & Fluid Animations: Clean glass cards, responsive layouts, smooth hover effects, and automatic portal theme matching. Modern UI
Version 3.2.3 Kaju Katli
Public Stable Baseline • Offline
Original Release: The first public release establishing the 100% offline attendance calculation foundation for students.
Features of Version 3.2.3
  • 100% Offline Processing: Pure local calculations inside your browser without any external network dependency.
  • Automatic Attendance Ingestion: Automatically calculated overall attendance metrics directly from your student portal dashboard.
  • Overall Margin Estimator: Simple margin counter showing total classes students could miss or needed to attend for 75% or 85%.
  • Basic Target Settings: Adjustable target threshold percentage (default 85%, configurable down to 75%).
Version 3.0.0 Gulab Jamun
Internal Beta • Not For Public
Why Not Released to Public: Early prototype testing phase. Unhandled network delays on slow campus WiFi caused synchronization race conditions and required full architecture restructuring.
Engineering Changes & Experimental Additions
  • Modular Component Structure: Divided single script into dedicated modules for calculation, UI cards, and storage.
  • Dynamic Portal Listener: Integrated automated state listeners for portal tab changes.
  • Floating Pill Widget: Early prototype of floating desktop overlay widget.
Version 2.2.0 Rabdi
Internal Milestone • Giving Back Pledge
The Equal Student Giving Back Pledge Ratified: Formal social commitment established to channel 100% of community voluntary contributions directly toward student welfare initiatives, Centre for Social Action (CSA), Anvaya, and UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 1 & SDG 4).
Milestones & Testing Scope
  • Transparent Public Ledger Blueprint: Designed the open-audit ledger system ensuring full community accountability.
  • Projected Remaining Classes: Added semester timetable duration estimation to calculate total classes remaining.
  • Why Not Public: UI layout clashes with the university navigation header required design revisions; tested within core team.
Version 2.0.0 Barfi
Internal Prototype • Not For Public
Why Not Released to Public: Mathematical formula inconsistencies when encountering non-standard elective courses and blended laboratory batches.
Prototype Features & Testing
  • Multi-Subject Table Reader: First trial of multi-course table calculation.
  • Storage Persistence Experiment: Tested caching raw percentage figures in browser local storage.
  • Target Slider Range: Added experimental range selector between 65% and 90%.
Version 1.5.9 Rasgulla
Pre-Release • UI Incompatibilities
Why Not Released to Public: Severe visual layout collisions with university portal styles. Lacked responsive viewport handling on laptop displays and had no multi-student data protection.
Early Experiments & Features

  • Color-Coded Status Tags: Simple Green (>85%), Yellow (75-85%), and Red (<75%) highlight tags.

Version 1.0.0 Agra Petha
Proof of Concept • Alpha MVP
Why Not Released to Public: Raw console script proof-of-concept tested locally. Lacked an extension wrapper, user interface, background sync, or multi-device support.
Foundational Proof of Concept
  • Core Calculation Math: First formula test of (Present Classes / Total Classes) * 100 on student tables.
  • Popup Output Dialog: Displayed calculated attendance margin in a standard browser dialog.
  • Feasibility Validation: Proved the viability of real-time student attendance forecasting on University portals.
