How Offline Cached Memory & Register Number Mapping Works

When you open your Christ University Portal, Attendance Analyzer saves a local snapshot of your attendance figures. This enables rich simulations and instant calculations without waiting for server responses.

Zero Portal Latency

Portal servers frequently experience load during peak morning hours. Cached data loads in 0.01 seconds without waiting for portal network requests.

Register No. Isolation

Cached states are strictly partitioned by your unique student Account Profile Key. Multiple accounts on the same laptop stay completely isolated.

100% Client-Side Privacy

All cached attendance figures remain strictly within your browser's private chrome.storage.local. No server ever sees or stores your data.

  • Manual Reset: Right-click the extension icon → Options → Click Reset Cache to purge stored snapshots.

3. Attendance Claims, 75% Rules & Calculation Formulas

According to Christ (Deemed to be University) academic regulations, students must maintain a minimum aggregate and subject-level attendance of 75% to be eligible to appear for End Semester Examinations (ESE). Attendance below 75% incurs exam debarment or mandatory remedial compensation hours.

Under Christ University policy, Medical Concessions (Blue Forms) operate under two strict conditions:
• Eligibility Window: Medical concessions can only be applied if your attendance after Yellow (co-curricular) claims is at least 75%.
• Upper Ceiling: Medical leave can only boost your attendance up to a maximum cap of 85%. If your attendance is already 85% or higher, medical concessions become inactive.

If your specific department, Deanery, or special academic circumstances grant an exemption allowing medical concessions to be applied unconditionally below 75% or beyond 85%, turning ON "Ignore 75% Rule for Medical Claims" in Extension Settings removes this safety restriction in the simulator calculations.

The calculator uses the mathematical formula:
Safe Leaves = floor((Attended Hours × 100 / Target %) - Conducted Hours)

• Positive Result: Displays the exact number of consecutive hours you can miss while maintaining your goal (e.g. 75% or 85%).
• Negative Result: Calculates the exact continuous recovery classes you must attend consecutively without missing any to lift your percentage back to your goal.

Yellow Form (Co-curricular) is for approved college fest, event, cultural, and sports duties. Approved yellow hours are added directly to your present attendance.

Blue Form (Medical) is for certified illness leaves. By university rule, medical concession can only be utilized if your attendance is between 75% and 85%.

Under Christ University regulations, medical concession cannot be claimed if your attendance falls below the mandatory 75% threshold. The extension automatically hides the slider and displays the status badge to ensure you don't calculate invalid claims.

The +1h tag indicates that the extension detected and automatically credited an approved claim from the portal's Absent Details table for that specific subject. You don't need to manually re-enter it!

Under Christ University regulations, Yellow Form concessions can only compensate for actual unexcused missed classes up to a maximum overall attendance ceiling of 100% (Attended + Claims ≤ Conducted Hours). The extension strictly caps the Yellow Claim slider dynamically so you cannot simulate claims beyond 100%, guaranteeing that your projections remain realistic, accurate, and compliant.

Every slider across the extension dashboard and subject simulator features precision iOS-style inline stepper controls:
• Single Click: Increments or decrements your hours by 1 immediately.
• Long Press (Hold): Holding down on + or − activates auto-repeat after 350ms, adjusting your hours at a fast, smooth rate of 3 steps per second (~333ms per step) until released or boundaries are reached.
• Direct Numerical Typing: You can also click directly on any value box and type your exact desired hours with the keyboard.

You can forecast how upcoming event duties or certified medical concessions will compensate for expected absences as the college conducts more classes throughout the semester.

4. Multi-User Storage, 5-Slot Limits & Advanced 900-Hour Option

The extension supports a strict limit of 5 student profiles per browser. Each profile maintains an isolated database slot containing the student's register number, name, course code, raw attendance, post-claim percentages, subject breakdowns, and last synced timestamp.

To protect memory integrity and avoid corrupted state or data bleed while browsing university pages, the extension locks the currently active student profile from single-card deletion. If you want to clear your current slot, you can:
• Open Settings → Reset Database → Hold "Clear All 5 Slots" for 5 seconds to reset all student slots safely.
• Or perform a full Factory Reset at the bottom of the Settings page.

When all 5 slots are occupied, a new student registration cannot be saved until a slot is freed. You can manage slots safely:
• Delete Inactive Profiles: Open Settings → Manage Users → Click the red trash icon on any inactive student card.
• Clear All 5 Slots (Hold 5s): In the Reset Database modal, press and hold the red button for 5 full seconds. An animated progress bar confirms the wipe.
• Factory Reset Extension (Hold 5s): At the very bottom of the Settings page, press and hold for 5 seconds to wipe all settings, student profiles, and database caches back to a freshly downloaded state.

When you are on pages like Absent Details or when the main attendance table is temporarily loading, the extension uses your last verified saved snapshot from local storage to display your figures instantly with zero waiting.

Yes! Every time you open or refresh the main /main/attendence page on the student portal, Attendance Analyzer reads the latest live numbers and automatically updates your stored local cache in real time.

Default Precision (100h / 300h): By default, subject sliders are capped at 100 hours and overall future prediction is capped at 300 hours for ultra-smooth 1-hour drag precision in standard semesters.

High-Credit & Annual Courses (900h Limit): If you are enrolled in an annual academic program, trimester structure, or high-credit lab curriculum where conducted hours exceed 300 hours:
1. Open Extension Settings (Right-click icon → Options or press Alt+S).
2. Under Advanced Customization, toggle ON "Maximum 900hr Simulation Limit".
3. All Future Predictor and Subject Simulator sliders will automatically scale their upper limit up to 900 hours.

If your semester subjects usually have between 30 to 80 conducted hours, keeping the option disabled is recommended for optimal slider drag sensitivity. Only enable the 900hr mode when your total course hours require high-capacity forecasting.
