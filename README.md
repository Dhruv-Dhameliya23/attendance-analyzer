# attendance-analyzer
📊 Attendance Analyzer
🎯 Attendance Predictor + Yellow & Blue Form Simulation

Predict your attendance before you miss a class.
Attendance Analyzer helps you understand your attendance, simulate future missed classes, and estimate how supported Yellow Form and Blue Form adjustments could affect your attendance.

🚀 What Can You Do?
📈 Attendance Predictor

Wondering what will happen if you miss a few more classes?

Use the interactive sliders to simulate:

📚 Missed classes
🏫 Conducted classes
📊 Estimated attendance after the change
🎯 Subject-wise attendance impact

Change the values and instantly see how your estimated attendance changes.

No manual calculations. No spreadsheets. Just adjust the slider and see the result.

🟡 Yellow Form Simulation

Have a supported Yellow Form claim?

Attendance Analyzer can help you simulate the possible attendance impact of the adjustment.

You can compare your attendance before and after the estimated adjustment to understand how it could affect your overall attendance.

⚠️ This is a calculation/simulation feature. The extension does not submit, approve, verify, or guarantee any claim.

🔵 Blue Form Simulation

The same calculation support is available for supported Blue Form scenarios.

You can estimate how an applicable adjustment could affect your attendance and understand the possible result before making decisions.

⚠️ Actual claim eligibility and approval are determined by your institution and its applicable policies.

📊 Attendance Dashboard

Get your important attendance information in one place.

Depending on the data available from the supported student portal, you can view:

📌 Attendance percentage
🏫 Total classes or hours conducted
✅ Hours attended
❌ Missed or absent hours
📚 Subject-wise attendance
📈 Attendance status and indicators

The dashboard turns the available attendance information into a cleaner and easier-to-understand view.

🔮 Predict Before You Miss

The biggest advantage of Attendance Analyzer is being able to test a scenario before it happens.

For example:

“If I miss 3 more classes, what will my attendance become?”

Move the predictor slider and instantly see the estimated result.

You can experiment with different scenarios instead of relying on guesswork.

🎚️ Smart Interactive Sliders

The predictor uses interactive sliders to make attendance planning quick and visual.

As you change the values:

🔄 Calculations update instantly
📊 Attendance changes visually
🎯 Related values automatically adjust when required
✨ Slider feedback makes changes easy to understand

The system maintains valid prediction conditions while you experiment with different attendance scenarios.

📚 Subject-Wise Attendance

Your overall attendance doesn't always tell the full story.

One subject might have a comfortable attendance percentage while another could be much closer to your required target.

Attendance Analyzer helps you view attendance subject by subject, where the required information is available.

This makes it easier to identify:

🟢 Subjects with comfortable attendance
🟡 Subjects that need attention
🔴 Subjects where additional absence could have a larger impact
🧮 Smart Attendance Calculations

Stop manually calculating attendance percentages.

Attendance Analyzer processes the attendance information available through the supported portal and performs the relevant calculations automatically.

Instead of repeatedly using formulas or spreadsheets, you can use one interface to understand your attendance and test different scenarios.

📝 Claim Adjustment Simulation

Attendance Analyzer can provide calculation support for supported claim categories and show the possible effect of an adjustment.

The tool can help you compare scenarios such as:

Before adjustment → Estimated result after adjustment

This makes it easier to understand how a potential adjustment could change your attendance.

However, the extension does not determine whether you are actually eligible for a claim.

🗓️ Plan Your Leaves Smarter

Before missing a class, you can use the predictor to understand the possible impact.

You can test different scenarios and see whether your estimated attendance remains within your desired range.

This can help when planning around:

✈️ Travel
🎓 Academic activities
🏆 Events
👥 Personal commitments
🗓️ Planned absences

Attendance Analyzer does not tell you whether you are permitted to take leave. It simply helps you understand the calculated attendance impact of the scenario you enter.
